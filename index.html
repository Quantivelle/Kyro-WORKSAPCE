How it works

From PDF to revision in three steps

No installation, no account, no server round-trip for your files. The heavy lifting happens on your own machine.

Step one

Drop your document

Add one or many PDFs by drag-and-drop or file picker. Text is extracted in the browser with pdf.js, preserving layout, font sizes and element structure.

Step two

Pick a mode

Choose summary, notes, quiz, flashcards, simplification, Q&A, study pack, gap analysis, exam optimizer, comparison or chat — and optionally add your own prompt.

Step three

Learn and revise

The response arrives as clean markdown with rendered maths, is sanitized before it touches the page, and can be saved to your local library instantly.

Privacy & security

Your documents never touch a server

Kyro WORKSAPCE has no backend, no database and no user accounts. The app is a set of static files, and everything that can be done on your device is done on your device.

  • PDFs are parsed in the browser with pdf.js — the file itself is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Notes live in IndexedDB and your API key in localStorage — both on your device only.
  • No analytics, no cookies, no telemetry and no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • All AI output is sanitized with DOMPurify before it is rendered, and a strict Content-Security-Policy is enforced.
  • One-click data wipe in Settings clears your key, notes, chats and consent record instantly.

FAQ

Questions, answered

The things people ask before they upload their first PDF.

Is Kyro WORKSAPCE really free?

Yes. The application is free, with no ads, no subscriptions and no paywalls. The only optional cost is the AI provider usage you pay for directly with your own OpenRouter API key — and many free OpenRouter models are available if you would rather pay nothing.

Do you upload my PDF or store my documents?

No. PDFs are parsed inside your browser with pdf.js, so your files are never uploaded to a Kyro WORKSAPCE server — because no such server exists. Your notes live in your browser’s IndexedDB and your API key in localStorage.

What actually leaves my device?

Only the text extracted from your PDF, and only when you trigger an AI action. It is sent directly from your browser to OpenRouter and authenticated with your own API key. Kyro WORKSAPCE never receives, stores or logs it. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No account is ever required. You can read, organise and search documents without a key. To generate AI output, add your own OpenRouter API key — it is saved only in your browser’s localStorage and can be deleted at any time.

Can I use it on a slow or low-end device?

Yes. The app ships a lite mode that switches on automatically on slow connections, low-memory devices and low-core phones, trimming shadows, blur and animation work. The tool is a single static page with no build step and no CDN dependency, so it also loads quickly on a weak connection.

Which AI modes are included?

Summary, question & answer, study notes, plain-English simplification, quizzes (MCQ, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, case study and HOTS), flashcards with active recall, revision and exam-brainstorm modes, a one-click study pack, knowledge gap analysis with a learning path, an exam optimizer, comparison tables, an AI chat assistant and optional self-review of the output.

Is the code open source?

Yes — the source code is released under the Apache License 2.0, as shown on the license page. The Quantivelle and Kyro WORKSAPCE names and the Quantivelle logo are trademarks and are not covered by that license; forks must rename the app and remove the branding, as described in the trademark guidelines.

Do I need an internet connection to study?

Only for AI generation. Reading PDFs, taking notes, searching your library, using flashcards and reviewing saved output all work offline once the page is loaded, because everything is stored locally in your browser.
